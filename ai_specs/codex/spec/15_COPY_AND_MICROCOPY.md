# Copy and Microcopy

Use **JobTrackerr!** consistently. Describe it as a web/mobile job-application tracker.

- Headline: `Your Career Journey, Organized.`
- Supporting line: `Organize applications, track progress, and never miss an opportunity.`
- Primary action: `Add Application`
- Views: `Board` and `List`
- Search: `Search by company or position`
- Empty result: `No applications found`
- Demo: `You're in Demo Mode. Results aren't saved.`
- Review: `Share Your Feedback`

Use exact statuses Applied, Interview, Offer, Rejected. Do not mention ApplyFlow, resume matching, automated follow-ups, or communication history as available.
